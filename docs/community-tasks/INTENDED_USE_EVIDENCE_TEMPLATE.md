# Intended-Use Evidence Template

**Community evidence record. Not a certification, formal qualification, or regulatory decision.**

[Contribute validation evidence](https://github.com/PhysioVerse-OSE/mps-validation-framework/issues/new?template=community-contribution.yml) · [Review project tasks](https://github.com/PhysioVerse-OSE/mps-validation-framework/issues)

Describe the intended use before summarizing model performance. Separate the evidence actually reported from proposed criteria, interpretations, and gaps.

## 1. Intended Use and Scope

| Item | Response |
|---|---|
| Model name, configuration, and version | To provide |
| Biological question or proposed decision supported by the model | To provide |
| Tissue, species, disease or exposure context | To provide |
| Intended population or application | To provide |
| Uses explicitly outside the scope | To provide |
| Source publication, dataset, protocol, and version | To provide |
| Contributor and review date | To provide |

## 2. Biological Relevance

Describe the mechanism, cell populations, functional behavior, and reference relevant to this use. Cite the supporting evidence and any disagreement between measurements. State whether similarity is supported at the transcriptomic, structural, functional, or other level; do not treat one level as proof of all others.

## 3. Performance and Comparison Evidence

| Endpoint or criterion | Reference or comparator | Reported method and metric | Reported result and uncertainty | Source location | Limitation or missing information |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| To provide | To provide | To provide | To provide | To provide | To provide |

For a proposed acceptance criterion, state who proposed it, when it was specified, why it fits the use, and whether it has actually been tested. Do not invent thresholds or apply an unexplained pass/fail classification.

## 4. Repeatability and Reproducibility

| Evidence area | What to record |
|---|---|
| Experimental units | Donors, devices, wells, cultures, or other independent units; distinguish repeated measurements |
| Within-study repeatability | Reported repeats, run conditions, metrics, and uncertainty |
| Between-batch variability | Cell lots, matrix or device lots, operators, batches, and reported effects |
| Cross-laboratory evidence | Participating settings, protocol transfer, relevant differences, and source-linked outcomes |
| Missing or failed measurements | Reported exclusion rules, failures, missingness, and their consequences |
| Analysis traceability | Public data, protocol, processing description, and version where available |

Use Not reported when the publication does not answer a question. Do not assume absence of an experiment.

## 5. Limits and Remaining Evidence Needs

Record boundary conditions, contradictory findings, transferability concerns, missing reference information, and additional experiments that would address the specific intended use. A proposal for further work is not a completed validation result.

## 6. External Status, Only When Documented

When a source reports formal qualification, certification, or regulatory use, record the issuing authority, exact status, stated context of use, date, version, and public source. Otherwise use Not documented. PhysioVerse community review does not grant or imply such status.

## Review and Attribution

Link the Issue or Pull Request, sources, reviewer comments, decision rationale, and any version into which accepted evidence is incorporated. Send Mostafa the submission link.
